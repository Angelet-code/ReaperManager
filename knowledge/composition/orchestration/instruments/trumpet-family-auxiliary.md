# Trumpet family: small D/Eb, piccolo, bass trumpet, cornet, flugelhorn

## Core idea
Default orchestral trumpet = C or Bb ([[trumpet]]). Reach for a relative only for a register or colour
the standard trumpet lacks. All are written in treble clef and transposed; MIDI stays at concert pitch.

| Instrument | Written → sounding | Sounding range (vault default; variants) | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small trumpet in D | +2 | 56–86 (Adler written F#3–D6 → 56–88) | small, brilliant, piercing |
| Small trumpet in Eb | +3 | 57–87 (Adler → 57–89) | as D |
| Piccolo trumpet in Bb | +10 (m7 up) | 64–91; effective 68–91 (written Bb3–A5) | highest, brightest |
| Bass trumpet in C / Bb | −12 / −14 | 42–72 / 40–70 | valve trombone |
| Bass trumpet in D / Eb | −10 / −9 | 43–74 / 44–75 (bottoms [?]) | Eb keeps trumpet quality |
| Cornet in Bb (A) | −2 (−3) | 52–84 (Adler); 52–82 (Piston) | between trumpet and horn |
| Flugelhorn in Bb | −2 | 52–82, optional 84 (Adler) | very sweet, opulent, horn-like |

## Rules
- R1 `PREFER` D, Eb or Bb piccolo for very high, brilliant lines and for Baroque clarino parts (Adler,
  Piston); same technique as the C trumpet.
- R2 `AVOID` their low register (below written C4; D trumpet below concert 62) and soft exposed highs: they
  turn shrill, mostly where they are used (Piston).
- R3 `[era: Piston 1955]` the D trumpet is rare in the orchestra; `[era: Adler 2002]` many players now
  choose it and it appears often. Modern default: allowed.
- R4 `MUST` bass trumpet parts in treble clef, transposed. In C or Bb it is a valve trombone, played by
  trombonists with a trombone mouthpiece; the Eb bass trumpet keeps a trumpet tone (large trumpet
  mouthpiece; 4th valve reaches concert Ab2 = 44) (Piston).
- R5 `ALLOWED` bass trumpet heroic (Wagner, Ride motive, f) or softly lyrical (Wotan's farewell, p).
- R6 `PREFER` the Eb bass trumpet for trumpet melodies below concert 52, or for old long-trumpet parts that
  go below the modern range (Strauss *Heldenleben*); otherwise give the line to a trombone.
- R7 `AVOID` treating the cornet as a trumpet (Piston): its tone sits between trumpet and horn, warm and
  mellow, without the heroic edge. Agility: Piston calls it the most agile brass; Adler, as agile as the
  trumpet and very precise in pitch.
- R8 `PREFER` cornets for folk-like, popular, march or military tunes, often in parallel 3rds/6ths
  (Tchaikovsky *Capriccio italien*, Stravinsky *Petrushka*).
- R9 `AVOID` cornets as regular orchestra members (Adler, Piston); modern cornet and trumpet sound almost
  alike (Piston). They stay common in brass bands and theatre orchestras.
- R10 `ALLOWED` flugelhorn for a sweet, horn-like solo in the trumpet register (Stravinsky *Threni*);
  common in bands, wind ensembles and jazz, rare in the orchestra (Adler). Funnel mouthpiece, larger bell.

## Period practice
- `[era: Berlioz, 19th-c. French]` 2 valved cornets carry the chromatic lines beside 2 natural trumpets
  (*Symphonie fantastique*); French scores kept cornets long after valve trumpets arrived (Piston).
- `[era: Rimsky-Korsakov]` *tromba contralta*: the F valve trumpet's fundamental with partials 2–9,
  ≈ concert 53–79 (inferred).
- `[era: early 20th c.]` flugelhorn sometimes played the high horn solo of Bach's *Quoniam* (Adler).
- "Bach trumpets" in high F/G are a misnomer: half-length tubes, so their 4th–8th partials sound as high
  as the old 8th–16th (Piston).

## Apply in MIDI / REAPER
- Track names `TPT D`, `PICC TPT`, `BASS TPT`, `CORNET`, `FLGH`, at concert pitch.
- No D/Eb patch: a C/Bb patch up to 84, a piccolo patch above. Keep the shrill top loud and short, or
  double it an octave lower with the C/Bb trumpets (inferred).
- Bass trumpet: a dedicated patch, else a trombone patch in 43–72 with trumpet-like accents (inferred).
- Cornet: a cornet patch, else a trumpet patch at moderate CC1 55–80 with softer accents (inferred).
- Flugelhorn: a flugelhorn patch at CC1 40–75 in 55–77; no patch: a mellow solo horn in 55–72 (inferred).

## Checklist
- [ ] Piccolo notes ≥ 64 (preferably ≥ 68); D/Eb used mainly ≥ 62.
- [ ] Bass trumpet transposition matches its key (C −12, Bb −14, D −10, Eb −9).
- [ ] Cornet/flugelhorn chosen for colour, not to replace a heroic trumpet line.

## Examples
- Baroque-like D trumpet line: 74 76 78 79 81 83 85 86 (D major scale, the old clarino register), f, trill on 81.
- Cornet duet in A major: C#5/A4 (73/69) → D5/B4 (74/71) → E5/C#5 (76/73), mf, parallel thirds.
