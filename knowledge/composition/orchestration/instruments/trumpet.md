# Trumpet (C, Bb, small D/Eb, bass trumpet)

## Core idea
Narrow tube, valves (since the 19th c.); today in **C** (non-transposing; more intensity and delicacy) or
**Bb** (sounds M2 = 2 st lower; keeps more fullness). Treble clef. The most penetrating attack of the brass.

| Instrument | Written → sounding | Full (sounding) | Practical |
|---|---|---|---|
| C trumpet | as written | 54–84 | 57–81 (general knowledge) |
| Bb trumpet | −2 | 52–84 (written F#3–D6) | 55–79 |
| Small trumpet in D / Eb | +2 / +3 | ≈ 56–86 / ≈ 57–87 [top approx.] | shrill, esp. high |
| Bass trumpet in C / Bb | −12 / −14 | 42–72 / 40–70 | played by trombonists |
| Flugelhorn (Bb) | −2 | ≈ 52–82 (general knowledge) | softer, darker cornet-like |

Registers (Bb, sounding): **52–59** low (rather dull; solemn, somewhat sombre) · **60–77** middle (clear,
bright, most articulate) · **78–84** high (brilliant but strident). For C trumpet add 2.

## Rules
- R1 `PREFER` melodies in the middle register, concert ≈ 60–79 (Hindemith *Concerto for Orchestra* I).
- R2 `AVOID` soft dynamics in the high register (≥ 78): penetrating and hard to play quietly.
- R3 `ALLOWED` low register for solemn, sombre colour, but it projects weakly.
- R4 `PREFER` fast staccato and accented repeated notes: more incisive than horn.
- R5 `AVOID` long stretches of double/triple tonguing: very tiring; give rests between bursts (Verdi *Aida* fanfares).
- R6 `ALLOWED` mostly valve trills; a few lip trills. `AVOID` low trills with awkward valve changes
  (written Ab3–Bb3, C4–Db4, B3–C#4).
- R7 `ALLOWED` mute (con sordina): cutting at f, distant echo at p (Mahler 1/IV).
- R8 `PREFER` roles: Classical = paired with **timpani** for cadential reinforcement and tutti filler;
  Baroque fanfares; 19th c. onward brilliant, agile melodic lines (Mahler 5 opening).
- R9 `ALLOWED` endurance (general knowledge): brass lips tire; in long cues alternate trumpets or leave
  ≥ 1 bar rest every ≈ 8 bars of playing, more after high passages.
- R10 `PREFER` doublings: trumpet + timpani on cadences; trumpet an octave above horn in fanfares;
  trumpet + Vln I or oboe for a bright melodic edge; trumpets + trombones in brass chorales (general
  knowledge for the last three).
- R11 `ALLOWED` small D/Eb trumpets for high, Baroque-like brilliance (shrill); bass trumpet = a valve
  trombone in timbre.

## Apply in MIDI / REAPER
- Tracks `Tpt 1`, `Tpt 2`, `Tpt 3` at concert pitch; written Bb part = MIDI +2.
- Articulations: sustain/legato, staccato, marcato, sfz, double-tongued repetitions (if the library has
  them), muted (straight/harmon), rips/falls (film/jazz), trills.
- Dynamics: velocity 70–120 for shorts; high notes (≥ 78) need CC1 ≥ 75; muted p echoes CC1 30–50.
- Fanfare: trumpets + timpani on tonic/dominant at cadences; see [[timpani]].
- Pan: centre-right rear.

## Checklist
- [ ] Bb trumpet notes 52–84 (C trumpet 54–84); most melodic notes 60–79.
- [ ] No p/pp (CC1 < 50, vel < 50) notes at ≥ 78.
- [ ] Repeated-16th tonguing bursts ≤ ≈ 2 bars, followed by rest.
- [ ] Rests distributed (≥ 1 bar per ≈ 8 bars in loud cues).
- [ ] Three trumpets in close harmony are within 60–81.

## Examples
- Cadential fanfare in D: D4–A4–D5 (62 69 74) with timpani A2/D3 (45/50).
- Muted echo: B4–G4 (71 67), p, straight mute.
