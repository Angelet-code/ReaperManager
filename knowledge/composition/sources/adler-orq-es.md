# Source card: Adler, El estudio de la orquestación

## Bibliographic info
- Samuel Adler, *El estudio de la orquestación*, Spanish translation of *The Study of Orchestration*,
  3rd edition (original 2002), Idea Books, two volumes (Part I instruments, Part II orchestration).
- Source id in frontmatter: `adler-orq-es` + **printed** pages (e.g. `adler-orq-es p.315-316`). The offset
  between scan and printed page varies (scan − 17 at the start, scan − 5 in the brass chapters, scan + 1
  from ch.11 on); each digest's frontmatter gives its exact mapping.

## Scope and era
- Late-20th-c. American/international orchestral practice, the most recent of the vault's orchestration
  books; it is the **modern default** when it disagrees with older authors (synthesis spec, principle 9).
  Piston (1955), UNIR and Romantic practice stay beside it as era-tagged variants; [[user-taste]] overrides
  all of them.
- Part I: every instrument family with range and register-colour charts, techniques, auxiliary and period
  instruments. Part II: orchestration by section and for full orchestra, accompaniment, transcription,
  score preparation, band. Appendix A: quick-reference range tables (professional, amateur and beginner
  ranges) and instrument names/terms in five languages.

## Reliability
- High for ranges, register charts and technique descriptions; the UNIR course already borrowed its charts.
- Range and register boundaries were read from small staff figures: values marked `[?]` in the digests
  (and in [[_instruments]] notes / `data/instruments.json`) are approximate and were not promoted to rules.
- Translation slips are corrected, not followed (e.g. harp G strings "blue" instead of F; Eb clarinet
  "below"; low-D trumpet "M7" in Appendix A; Italian/French term misprints).
- Musical examples were described, not transcribed; the notes use re-composed examples.
- **Missing printed pages** (absent from the scan, not reconstructed): 86–87 (double bass examples),
  92–93 (harp glissando/arpeggio examples), 158–159 and 161 (start of piano → strings transcription,
  Fauré *Requiem*), 210–211 (Eb/D clarinet text), 222–223 (bassoon registers and examples), 342–345
  (trombone range, positions, registers), 361–362 (early brass uses in ch.11).
- **Filled:** 342–345 from the English 2nd edition, printed p.312–315 → [[adler-orq-en2]].
- Not digested: p.48 (end of the artificial-harmonic repertoire list), Appendix A after p.796 (term list
  continues on p.797), Appendix B (bibliography). Printed p.127–128 are only thinly covered by the ch.5
  digest.
