# Source card: Hindemith, Armonía tradicional II

- **Work:** Paul Hindemith, *Armonía tradicional*, vol. 2 — *Ejercicios de armonía para cursos superiores*
  (Spanish translation of the exercise book for *A Concentrated Course in Traditional Harmony*). Scanned
  copy, 66 scan pages; printed page = scan page + 5.
- **Scope:** not a theory text. Exercises 1–5 correspond to vol. 1 chapters IX–XIII (chord vocabulary per
  stage, no modulation); exercise 6 adds modulation and teaches the planning method on a suite for string
  orchestra, then applies it to fugues, sonata movements and variations.
- **Style:** common-practice vocabulary, but the instructions on texture, accompaniment, accent by density
  and planning are general compositional craft and are marked `universal` where used.
- **Reliability:** high for the stated instructions and the planning example (bar numbers, keys, density,
  voice counts). The numeric 0–3 tension/oscillation scales in the notes are an inferred quantification of
  Hindemith's rankings. Piece sketches themselves were not transcribed.
- **Digest:** `sources/digests/hindemith-2--p001-066.md`.
