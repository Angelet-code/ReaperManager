# 4-Day Challenge (film-scoring deck)

## Bibliographic info
- Workshop slide deck, "4-Day Challenge", in English. Slides 1–10 (slide 1 is a title). Author and
  date are not stated on the slides.
- Source id: `film-4day-challenge`. Notes cite `slide N`.

## Scope
- Slide 2: harmony → emotion (diatonic / non-diatonic, extensions, clusters, harmonic rhythm).
- Slide 3: storytelling with functions (I, IV, vi/iii/ii, V without third, vii°).
- Slides 4, 7, 8: template (fast action patches, strings basics, orchestral enhancers).
- Slide 5: counterpoint as separation or association (main and secondary parameters).
- Slide 6: doubling (by colour, construction, decay, emotion; typical bass doubling; four-step method).
- Slide 9: reverb types (panoramic vs impact; separate reverbs; basses dry).
- Slide 10: the five elements (direction, movement, background, enhancer, bass).
- Style scope: **film / trailer / media scoring with sample libraries**. These are not common-practice
  rules.

## Reliability
- Practitioner advice, consistent with standard film-scoring practice. Short bullets, so the notes expand
  them. Concrete numbers (reverb times, send levels, CC values) are **not** in the deck. The notes add
  them as `(general knowledge)` defaults.
- "Emotion" mappings are tendencies for media music, not universal laws.
