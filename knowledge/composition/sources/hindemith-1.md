# Source card: Hindemith, Armonía tradicional I

- **Work:** Paul Hindemith, *Armonía tradicional*, vol. 1 — Spanish translation of *A Concentrated Course
  in Traditional Harmony*, Book I (original German/English 1943). Scanned copy, 132 scan pages; printed page
  = scan page − 7.
- **Scope:** four-part chord connection from triads to modulation, plus a final chapter of accompaniment
  exercises (four sonatinas). No literature examples; exercises are Roman-numeral drills, figured basses
  and melodies in all keys.
- **Style:** common-practice harmony (roughly 1600–1900). Not a style course: rules are school-exercise
  rules, stated strictly and then relaxed. The notes record the *final* (relaxed) state and mark the early
  strict state as a `strict` checker mode.
- **Reliability:** high for rules and chord spellings. Some exercise pitches in the scans are partly
  illegible (marked `[?]` in the digests); note examples are re-composed, not copied. A few resolutions
  (deceptive-cadence doubling, 13th resolution, seventh resolution as explicit rule) are filled from
  standard practice and marked `(general knowledge)` in the notes.
- **Digests:** `sources/digests/hindemith-1--p001-044.md`, `hindemith-1--p045-088.md`,
  `hindemith-1--p089-132.md`.
