# HTTYD "Test Drive" (3m20)

## Bibliographic info
- John Powell, *How to Train Your Dragon* (DreamWorks, 2010), cue 3m20 "Test Drive" (1j), full score
  excerpt, orchestration by John Ashton Thomas. 15 pages, bars 1–71; the cue continues after the excerpt.
- Source id: `httyd-test-drive`. Scan pages = printed pages; notes cite `p.N`.

## Scope
- Orchestration case study, not a rulebook. Forces: a3 woodwinds + whistle + contrabass clarinet, 12
  horns, 4 trumpets, 4 + 2 trombones, tuba, timpani + 6 percussion, celesta, 2 harps, divided strings.
- Content: tempo/key/harmony map, an 11-section bar-by-bar description, techniques by category, and 22
  transferable techniques (T1–T22) phrased as rules.
- Style scope: **modern film adventure scoring** (Celtic colour, big brass). It is not common-practice
  harmony; the pedal + chromatic-mediant writing is idiomatic film language.

## Reliability
- Structure, entries, rhythms, dynamics and chord symbols were legible on all pages. The chord-symbol
  staff makes the harmony reliable.
- Exact pitches of dense woodwind runs (b.25–29, b.46–54), brass cluster spellings (b.40–44), and some
  trombone and high-string pitches are approximate (`[?]` in the digest). The notes use them only as
  illustrative registers.
- The rules are generalisations from one cue. They apply to the adventure/heroic idiom and should be
  scoped as such.
