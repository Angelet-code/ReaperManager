# Compose or arrange a modern song

1. **Taste first.** Read [[user-taste]]. For a cover, get the real chart and the reference (R8). Genre
   specifics (drums, swing, bass technique) live in the Claude memory files; ask if the feel is unclear.
2. **Structure.** [[form-for-modern-songs]] maps verse/pre/chorus/bridge onto phrase and section
   functions. Plan bar counts, where energy rises, and where the space (drops) goes. Make regions.
3. **Harmony.**
   - Keep the progression the style expects.
   - Borrow craft only where it serves the song: a secondary dominant into the chorus
     ([[secondary-dominants]]), modal mixture for colour ([[mode-mixture-and-neapolitan]]), a
     pivot-chord key lift for the last chorus ([[modulation]]).
   - [[harmony-emotion-mapping]] helps translate mood words into chords.
4. **Voicing pads, keys and strings.** Use [[harmony-voicing-for-instruments]] and
   [[voice-leading-basics]]: common tones held, stepwise inner voices, no muddy low thirds. Parallel
   fifths are fine in guitar power chords, so don't "correct" them.
5. **Orchestral layers** (strings or brass on choruses, as in the cover projects):
   - [[texture-accompanied-melody]] and [[doubling-colour-and-decay]];
   - instrument notes for ranges ([[violin]], [[cello]], [[horn]], …);
   - entries below the climax level ([[dynamics-by-orchestration]]).
6. **Arrangement audit.** Run [[five-elements-arrangement-checklist]] per section (direction, movement,
   glue, enhancer, bass). Each chorus should differ from the verse in at least two of: register,
   density, rhythm, lead instrument.
7. **Validate.** Run [[validate-generated-midi]].
