# Glossary — musical form

False friends first:
- **puente** — in sonata form = transition between themes A and B; in pop = bridge / middle 8. See
  [[sonata-form]], [[form-for-modern-songs]].
- **período / frase** — Zamacois's *período* = 4-bar unit (English "phrase"), *frase* = 8-bar unit (English
  "period/sentence"). See [[phrase-structure]].
- **cadencia** — harmonic cadence, but also the soloist's **cadenza** in a concerto ([[concerto-form]]).
- **estribillo** — refrain (rondo, song) and, in pop, the **chorus**.
