# Case study: HTTYD "Test Drive" (3m20)

## Core idea
Score: John Powell, orchestrated by John Ashton Thomas, in concert pitch. The forces are big film
brass: **12 horns**, 4 trumpets, 4 trombones + 2 bass trombones and tuba. Woodwinds are a3 with a folk
whistle and a contrabass clarinet. Percussion has 7 staves, plus celesta, 2 harps and divided strings.
The cue is a model of **constant motion with a fixed floor**: the harmony and colour change above
pedals and ostinati, and every return of a theme is re-scored.

## Rules (transferable techniques)
Each rule is detailed in the linked note.
- R1 `PREFER` A theme is always a multi-family unison; only the solo colour ornaments
  ([[heroic-theme-orchestration]]).
- R2 `PREFER` Heroic tune = horns in unison + Vln II, trombone triads, half-note roots below.
- R3 `PREFER` Each return re-scores the theme (solo → tutti; horns → oboes/trumpets with a violin
  motor).
- R4 `PREFER` A tonic pedal re-attacked every 2–4 bars, with fixed ostinati and harmony moving above
  ([[action-ostinato-pedal-gallop]]).
- R5 `PREFER` A gallop in all the low instruments + timpani + snare, with chords changing only in the
  brass.
- R6 `PREFER` Woodwinds in hocket or beat-groups; violas and celli interlocked.
- R7 `PREFER` Grow by octave layers, not by dynamics (the pedal itself decays ff → f).
- R8 `PREFER` (this style) A cymbal on every phrase downbeat, with a swell before it
  ([[film-percussion-phrase-marking]]). For this user, prefer cymbal swells, timpani rolls or a silence before the downbeat; snare rolls only in rock/marching contexts or on explicit request ([[user-taste]] R3).
- R9 `PREFER` Hold the trumpets back until the build's last beat
  ([[building-and-releasing-climaxes]]).
- R10 `PREFER` A one-bar sextuplet sweep with rolls and a harp glissando into big downbeats.
- R11 `ALLOWED` A stall on a bVI pedal with chromatic clusters and aleatoric glissandi for 4–5 bars.
- R12 `PREFER` Step the click up (+1.5, +1.5, +3) and lift the key a whole step for the final statement
  (the key lift only when the user has not fixed the key).
- R13 `PREFER` After the climax, restart at subito f, and end with secco hits.
- R14 `AVOID` Celesta or glockenspiel in ff tutti; they belong to the light ostinato sections.

## Apply in MIDI / REAPER
To model a cue on it:
1. Create markers from the section map, scaled to the scene length.
2. Build the template subset: HN a6 legato and long, TPT short and long, LOW BRASS, WW LONG/SHORT +
   FL RUNS, CELESTA, HARP, TIMP, SNARE, BD, CYMBALS, SUS CYM, VLN I/II, VLA, VC (two tracks: melody and
   bass), CB ([[orchestral-mockup-template]]).
3. Write the pedal and engine first, then the themes, then the enhancers.
4. Validate with [[five-elements-arrangement-checklist]] and [[orchestration-errors-checklist]].

## Checklist
- Does the cue have a fixed low floor (pedal or gallop) under moving harmony?
- Is every theme statement re-scored on return?
- Are the trumpets held until the first big arrival?
- Are tempo and key raised for the final statement (key only if the user has not fixed it)?
- Do phrase downbeats get percussion marks?
